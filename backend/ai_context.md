# BUSINESS PERMIT APPLICATION KNOWLEDGE BASE

## Overview
A Business Permit (Mayor's Permit) is a legal authorization issued by the Local Government Unit (LGU) that allows a person or business entity to operate legally within the municipality or city.

Before a business permit is issued, applicants must submit the required documents and obtain clearances from various government offices. The exact requirements depend on the nature of the business and local government policies.

==========================================================
GENERAL APPLICATION PROCESS
==========================================================

STEP 1 - Register the Business
The business must first be legally registered.
Depending on the business type:
• Sole Proprietorship → DTI Registration
• Corporation → SEC Registration
• Cooperative → CDA Registration

The applicant must also obtain:
• BIR Certificate of Registration
• Tax Identification Number (TIN)

STEP 2 - Secure Barangay Clearance
Office: Barangay Hall where the business is located.
Typical Requirements:
• Business Registration
• Valid ID
• Lease Contract or Proof of Ownership
• Application Form

STEP 3 - Submit Business Permit Application
Submit the Unified Application Form to the Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO).
Typical Documents (as seen on the Municipality of Roxas Unified Form):
1. Annual Income Tax Return (AITR) (FOR RENEWAL OF BUSINESS)
2. Photocopy of DTI Certificate/SEC/CDA
3. Community Tax Certificate (Sedula)
4. Barangay Business Clearance
5. Sanitary Permit/Health Clearance (Rural Health Center)
6. Environment & Sanitation Certification (MENRO)
7. Zoning Compliance Certificate/Locational Clearance (MPDO)
8. Building Permit/Occupational Permit (MEO)
9. Tourism Clearance (Resort, Hotel, Restaurant & Lodging) (Tourism Office)
10. Payment Receipt (MTO)
11. Fire Safety Inspection Clearance (BFP)

==========================================================
OFFICE RESPONSIBILITIES
==========================================================

BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION (BFP)
Purpose: Ensures compliance with the Fire Code.
Inspection includes: Fire Extinguisher, Exit Signs, Emergency Exit, Electrical Wiring.

MUNICIPAL HEALTH OFFICE (MHO/RHU)
Purpose: Ensures compliance with sanitation and public health standards.
Issues: Sanitary Permit / Health Clearance.

MUNICIPAL ENGINEERING OFFICE (MEO)
Purpose: Verifies structural safety.
Issues: Building Permit / Occupational Permit.

MUNICIPAL PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT OFFICE (MPDO)
Purpose: Checks zoning compliance.
Issues: Zoning Clearance.

MUNICIPAL TREASURER'S OFFICE (MTO)
Purpose: Assessment and payment of taxes and fees.
Issues: Official Receipt.

MENRO
Purpose: Environmental compliance.
Issues: Environment & Sanitation Certification.

TOURISM OFFICE
Issues: Tourism Clearance (Required only for Hotel, Resort, Inn, Pension House, Tourist Attraction, Restaurants).

AI RESPONSE GUIDELINES
==========================================================
1. Ask what type of business the applicant is registering.
2. Ask whether the application is NEW or RENEWAL.
3. Recommend only the permits generally required for that business type based on the list above.
4. Explain why each permit is required.
5. Inform users that requirements may vary depending on LGU policies.
6. Never assume every business needs every permit.
7. Distinguish between Required, Conditionally Required, and Usually Not Required.
8. If unsure, advise the applicant to verify with the Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO) of Roxas, Oriental Mindoro.
